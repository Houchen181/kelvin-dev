Also asked for (2026-10-24): try a few more instruments of different kinds and see whether it works well. Status: done, in Kelvin 0.3.22. Tried the same way (real manuals from the makers' sites, simulated instruments written from those manuals, cards written from Kelvin's prompts by Claude in a development session) with a Lake Shore 372 resistance bridge (serial, 57600 7O1), a Rigol DP832 power supply, a Tektronix AFG31252 function generator and a Pfeiffer TPG 362 gauge controller (no identity reply, its own protocol). All four end with the card tried, the instrument added beside the others with only reads sent, and the first output change asking. What the tests found and fixed:
- **Choosing the pages.** In 200-page programming manuals the excerpt was the contents and the index, or missed the output pages. Pages are now ranked by the commands they define, with outputs, levels and readings first. A command's class comes from its first node (OUTPut:STATe is an output), deep commands count less, channel numbers are read as the bare command, and Lake Shore's labelled commands count.
