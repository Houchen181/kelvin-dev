---
name: check-a-result
description: Before reporting a result or drawing a conclusion: check that it is real and give its uncertainty.
---
1. Look at the data themselves (look_at, get_run_data), not only the fit or the summary.
2. Reproduce it: repeat under the same conditions; the spread between repeats is part of the uncertainty.
3. Sweep the other way, or slower: hysteresis, drift and thermal lag show up as a difference.
4. Controls: the excitation off, another contact pair or channel, a reference sample, the drive off.
5. Instrument limits: no overload, no compliance reached, no range change in the middle, no saturation.
6. Units and factors: rms against peak, preamplifier gains, dividers, the series resistor's value.
7. The fit: residuals without structure, a fair reduced χ², the parameters' uncertainties, and a model that fits the physics.
8. Then say it: the value ± its uncertainty, the conditions, and what was checked.
