---
name: transport-iv
description: Measuring I–V curves or resistance of a device safely: limits, compliance, two- or four-probe, gates.
---
1. Ask the person for the device's limits (current, voltage, gate leakage, breakdown) and set the source's compliance at or below them.
2. Start small and sweep from 0 outward, slowly, then back to 0; no jumps in the bias.
3. Two-probe includes the contacts and the leads; use four-probe for the sample's own resistance.
4. Gates: check the leakage on a small sweep first, and stop if the gate current rises above the person's limit (often about 1 nA).
5. A point at compliance is not data: lower the range or the bias rather than raise the compliance.
6. Rewiring, grounding the sample and touching it are the person's to do: ask, and keep sources at safe meanwhile.
