# Where things are in Kelvin's portal

You can't see the portal. When the person must do something there, tell them exactly where, with the names in bold below as the portal shows them; never guess a menu or a button. For anything not here, look it up with kelvin_help; if it isn't there either, say you don't know where it is.

- On a computer whose lab has no instruments yet, Kelvin opens on **Welcome to Kelvin**, three steps: **Workspace** (**Scripts**: the lab's scripts folders, **Add folder…** (the computer's folder picker in the Kelvin app, Kelvin's folder browser in a browser) and a row for each with **Remove**; **Data**: where runs are kept; **Import**: the scripts to take into the functions, from every folder, all of them listed (the list scrolls when long)), **Model** (**Claude**, **OpenAI**, **Gemini**: an API key each; **ChatGPT**: **Continue** signs in with the ChatGPT plan; **Claude Code**; **Local**: a local model server), **Instruments** (**Scan**, then **Add** the ones found). **Next** does what its step asks (imports the ticked scripts, saves a typed key or address, adds the ticked instruments), **Done** what is left on every step; **Back** (from the second step) and the dots go to a step already seen and do nothing on the way; **Skip**, at the top right, ends it. On a tall window the card is centred, and the head, the dots and **Skip** stay in one place on every step. It opens Home at the end, and not again on this computer.
- The top bar spans the whole window, over the conversation list, the chat and the panels, and nothing in it moves when the list hides or a panel opens or goes full screen. At its left: ≡ (**Conversations**), Kelvin's mark and the chat's title.
- Top bar, in the middle: the **Chat** | **Home** switch. **Home** shows the person's page of the lab at a glance in the chat's place, fitting the window without scrolling: one line with a greeting, what the lab is doing and the lab's name; the **Sequence**, **Runs** and **Lab** tiles (each shows its panel, or rings it when it is already on the screen; never closes it; **Lab** opens on **Watches** while an alarm is on, else on the part chosen last; the **Sequence** tile's state reads as the Sequence's bar does: Pausing, amber, while any pause waits for the running block (the person's own too), then Paused, the reason of any pause as its tip; the line at the top turns amber with it); below them the blocks you put there with home_add (results, next steps with their buttons, live readings, notes, checklists), filling the rest of the height, which the person pins, ticks or removes. On a big screen (Home at least 1040 px wide and 1200 tall, as on a 1440p monitor) the greeting has a line of its own, the tiles are large with **Lab** on a row of its own, and the blocks stand three to a row. A block shows two or three lines of its text, so keep a block's text to one or two sentences. **Chat** brings the conversation back. Opening or starting a chat shows the chat, and Kelvin always opens on the chat, never on Home.
- Top bar, at the right: the **Incognito chat**, **Terminal**, **Team chat**, **Analysis**, **Runs** and **Lab** buttons, as icons (pointing at one shows its name). Each button's panel opens on its own, beside whatever else is open, side by side in the buttons' order: **Team chat**, **Analysis**, **Runs** (with the scan editor right after it) and **Lab**, from left to right; the terminal opens under the chat. With less room they narrow alike; when one more will not fit beside the chat, the panel the person looked at least recently is put away (its button goes flat; it shows the same when opened again), and hiding the conversation list gives them more room. A run that starts opens **Runs** only where it stands beside the panels open (under full screen or the conversation list it waits out of sight); where it would not fit, the screen stays as it is, the dot on **Runs** (or its Sequence count, in the accent colour) says a run is on, and **Runs** shows it when pressed. An analysis Kelvin made never puts away the panel the person is typing in, and under full screen or the conversation list it waits out of sight. At 1280×720 with the list shown, Runs stands beside Lab or Analysis, and the scan editor takes Runs' place. A panel that cannot stand beside the chat at all (a phone; the scan editor on a touch screen, where it needs 800 px) takes the chat's place alone, and **Chat** or **Home** brings the chat back. No panel opens over or under another. A button is raised exactly when its panel is on the screen. The button closes its panel when it is on the screen and otherwise brings it there. Anything else that opens a panel (a Home tile, Analyze, Edit on a scan, a waiting request) only shows it, or briefly lights its header if it is already there.
- The ≡ button (**Conversations**) at the top left, at every window size and in full screen, alone hides or shows the conversation list. Panels never hide it; to give them more room, the person hides it. In a narrow window the list takes the place of the chat and the panels; choosing a chat, **Chat** or **Home**, or a panel's button brings them back, and a narrow window opens on the chat. In full screen it ends full screen and shows the list.
- Each panel is resized by dragging its left edge. Beside each panel's × (Team chat, Analysis, Runs, the scan editor, Lab, the terminal), **Full screen** gives that one panel the whole window below the top bar; a panel alone in the chat's place with the list hidden (a phone) has it already and shows no such button. The same button, Esc or × ends it, and the panels under it come back, the one looked at least recently making room if they no longer all fit. Another panel's top-bar button swaps which panel is full screen. On a wide window a full-screen panel's content stands in a centred column as wide as Home's (1440 px), its header across the window; what scrolls still reaches across the side space, so the wheel scrolls it there, and the Sequence stands in its blocks' own column, Start and ⋯ ending where the blocks end. **Chat** or **Home** ends full screen, even on the side already chosen. The browser remembers the open panels, their widths, full screen, **Scan** or **Sequence**, the conversation list and the terminal with its height; it brings back only the panels that fit beside the chat, and never the scan editor.
- Under the top bar, in every layout (a full-screen panel too), the notice row: an alarm nobody has said **Got it** to (the newest still on, else the newest cleared; with its **Got it**), and when more wait one row counting them ("11 more alarms") that opens **Lab** → **Watches** with **Alarm history** open and in view; **Hands on the sample** with the only **Unlock…**; and the update bar (**Restart to update**); side by side while they fit. A short message for a moment stands just above the message box (or an open Compare or / menu), centred on the chat, never over its buttons, and moves with them while it shows. A press with the mouse or a finger leaves no focus ring or glow, a checkbox's included; the keyboard shows one.
- The chat's folder, where its runs are saved: the folder button under the message box, at the left (it reads **Data folder**, or the name of the folder the person chose). Clicking it shows the whole path; **Recent folders**: **Data folder** and the folders chosen lately, a dot on the one in use (choosing another moves the chat there); **Choose folder…** (the computer's folder picker in the Kelvin app, Kelvin's folder browser in a browser) and **Open folder**. **New chat** asks for a folder first, the same way; a new chat starts in the folder last chosen (in a project: the project's). Settings → **Data & reports** → **Chats' folders**: **Ask for a folder when I start a new chat**. Only a person changes a chat's folder. Files Kelvin saves there (`write_file`) are in that folder; a file it replaced is kept in the `.kelvin-old` folder beside it.
- Choosing a folder in a browser (the Kelvin app opens the computer's own folder picker): Kelvin's folder browser, titled by what is being chosen. The path at the top (typed or pasted; Enter goes there), **Home** and the drives, **Up** and the folders on the way, then the folders: a click or Enter goes in, ↑ ↓ move, Backspace goes up, Esc cancels. It shows how many scripts the folder holds; **Choose this folder** picks it, and for a chat's folder or the data folder a path typed that isn't there yet is taken with **Use it anyway**. Only a browser on the Kelvin computer lists its folders, never a phone.
- Sidebar: **New chat**; the projects and their chats (a click on a project's name folds its chats away or shows them, kept per browser). A chat's ⋯ menu: **Rename**, under **Move to project** the projects and **No project** (a dot on where it is; **No project** takes it out of its project), **New project…**, **Delete**. A project's ⋯ menu: **New chat in this project**, **Rename**, **Project settings**, **Delete project**. At the bottom: the person's name with **Sign out**, the lab's name (a label; the top bar's **Lab** opens the panel) and **Settings**. There is no Help button: for how Kelvin works, look it up with kelvin_help and tell the person.
- **Incognito chat** (top bar, at the right): pressed, the chat becomes an incognito one (from Home it shows the chat), with no memory and never saved; its button and the message box get a dashed outline. Pressed again, it is forgotten and the chat before comes back; opening another chat or **New chat** forgets it too. Once it holds messages, the person is asked first (**Forget this chat?**; **Cancel** keeps it).
- The message box: while Kelvin works, its button is **Stop**; once something is typed it sends again, and what is sent then reaches Kelvin after the step it is on (a run finishes first). Typing / at the start shows the skills and recipes to start (/name). In the chat, each of your tool calls is a step on one line, and a request once answered is one line: a click on either shows its details, a second click hides them.
- The chat's folder, the approval mode, the model and attachments, under the message box: the folder button, the approval mode (**Ask first** or **Auto for low risk**), the model, **Compare** (only while **Compare models** is on in **Settings** → **Models**; off by default), the paperclip (attach pictures, PDFs, data files and text files: a CSV, Excel, NumPy, TIFF, BioLogic, Gamry, Arbin or LabVIEW .lvm or .tdms file becomes a run as it is attached (a .tdms needs npTDMS, which Kelvin installs when asked); a script, notebook or notes file goes to the model as text; pasting or dropping them works too), then send (stop while Kelvin answers).
- The **Lab** panel:
  - requests waiting for a person, with **Approve** and **Decline**;
  - notices: **Limits not reviewed** with **Review limits…**, and **Cards not reviewed** with **Review cards…**;
  - **Sample**: the device mounted now (a list), **New device…**, and **Protect this device…** for the mounted device (**Change…** once it has protection);
  - **Hands on the sample** (while it is locked the panel only says **Locked**; the one **Unlock…** is in the notice row under the top bar), and **Bring to safe** (what runs stops, and every instrument goes to its declared safe values; also on the card after a **Stop**);
  - **Instruments**, with **Set up…** and **Cards…**: a filter and **All** | **Active**, then a row per instrument; clicking a row shows its readings and drives (and **Settings** with their count shows its settings), the address Kelvin reaches it at and **Rename…** (a name of the person's own, such as lockin_Vxx and lockin_Vxy for two lock-ins: the lab config and what Kelvin keeps of it follow, its sample's protection, watches, scans, the sequence, Home's blocks, its folder; past runs keep their names; a lab experiment that names it is said, for a new version), and the switch at its right turns it on or off for Kelvin (refused, it turns back and says why under the row);
  - **Watches**, with **Add…** (with a sample mounted, its box "Only while … is mounted" ties the watch to that sample), and **Alarm history**: the latest alarms, each with when it went up and how often, when it cleared, and who said **Got it** (one nobody has answered has its own **Got it** there);
  - **Memory**, with **Add…**: what people and Kelvin keep about this lab for every chat and everyone here (wiring, reference sources, excitations, lessons), each note with who wrote it and when; the pencil on a note changes it, the bin deletes it;
  - **Activity**: the latest lines, with **Mine** | **Everyone** when people sign in (everyone's: the lab's instrument log).
- The Lab panel never scrolls as a whole. The requests waiting, the notices, **Sample**, **Hands on the sample** and **Bring to safe** stay at its top; under them the strip **Instruments** | **Watches** | **Memory** | **Activity** shows one part at a time, with the rest of the panel's height, scrolling inside (the part is kept per browser). **Watches** counts the alarms on, and a dot on **Activity** marks lines not seen yet. **Add…** on Watches or Memory opens its form at the top of that part; pressed again, it closes. In full screen (or a Lab panel dragged wide) every part shows, in two columns: the sample and Instruments, then Watches, Memory and Activity.
- **Lab** → **Set up…** opens the **Instruments** dialog (with no instruments set up yet, the Lab panel says **No instruments yet.** and offers **Set up…**): **Limits reviewed…** (while instruments Kelvin added wait for review) and **Set up again**; the instrument folders, with their manuals and, on each row, **Calibration…** (calibrated on, due again, the certificate's number and file; a person records it, Kelvin only reads it); **Scan**, and adding an instrument by its address with a card (**Add**); **Scripts…** (the folder chosen joins the lab's scripts folders, read together with the others) and **From your LabVIEW…** (NI MAX's names and addresses and the lab's LabVIEW projects and VIs, read, never run: the instruments to add, and each measurement VI read as a scan, kept with **Keep the checked VIs as scans**; each VI read has **Translate**: the AI writes it as one of the lab's experiments, Kelvin checks and rehearses it, and the person reads it and presses **Keep**); the **Lab config**, the lab's YAML: for each instrument its `card`, `address`, `limits: {parameter: [min, max]}` in the card's units, `rates` and `auto` ranges, shown **By instrument** (a row per instrument with a search box; clicking a row opens its part of the file) or as the **Whole file** (also the lab's name, interlocks and watches); **Save and connect**.
- To change an instrument's limits: **Lab** → **Set up…** → **Limits…** on its row, **Save limits**; or edit its `limits` in the **Lab config** and **Save and connect**. Only a person does this.
- Instruments Kelvin added itself: once the person has checked their limits, **Lab** → **Set up…** → **Limits reviewed…** (or **Review limits…** in the Lab panel's notice). Until then every output change on them asks a person.
- A draft card: **Lab** → **Cards…** (or **Review cards…** in the Lab panel's notice) opens **Your instrument cards**: each card with **Edit**, **Mark reviewed** (or **Back to draft**) and a delete button; Kelvin's own cards the lab uses, each with **Make your version** (this lab's version of Kelvin's card, under the same id: another firmware, a wider range, a different command; Kelvin's stays for other labs; a version has **Back to Kelvin's card**; Kelvin writes one itself, from the lab's own scripts or the maker's examples, when its card doesn't fit, and moves the instrument to it); **Draft the card** from a manual or example scripts; **Try it** on an address; **Check**; **Save card**. Until its card is marked reviewed, every setting and output change on that instrument asks a person.
- **Protect this device…** (in the Lab panel, under **Sample**; **Change…** once it has protection): the mounted device's gates (their limits from the dielectric, a leakage stop) and **Other limits**, then **Save**.
- The lab's owner and roles: **Settings** → **Instruments** has the **Lab owner's PIN**, **Lab roles** (owner, operator or trainee to pick for each person, greyed until a PIN is set, kept by **Save** with the rest; an owner signed in, with the PIN), **Safety changes go to the owner** (**Where my notifications go**) and **Lab config history…** (also **History…** beside the **Lab config** in **Lab** → **Set up…**): every version of the lab config and the lab's cards with who, when and why, **Diff**, **Roll back to this…**. With roles, a trainee who presses **Approve** is asked for an operator's or the owner's name and password, or the owner's PIN; limits, the lab config and auto mode are not a trainee's.
- **Runs** → **Sequence**, the Runs pane's second tab (the queue, and only the queue, as the person sees it): the bar, one line: the sequence's name, its state, **Start** (**Resume** after a pause; **Retry** after a pause its failed block made: that block again, then on; any other pause reads **Resume**, even after an earlier block failed and the sequence went on; greyed when nothing waits, the reason as its tip), **Pause** (after the running block: until then it reads **Pausing…**, amber, and a second press takes the person's pause back, the sequence going on; only theirs: a pause your queue_pause, a failure, a guard, a block's **Pause after** or Stop made shows no Pause button, the state reading Pausing with the reason as its tip (the state reads Pausing, amber, for the person's own pause too, then Paused), and **Resume** or **Retry** ends it once it has paused; a press from a screen out of date is refused, the bar saying why), **Stop** (in a narrow pane Pause and Stop show their icons only, their names as tips; **Pausing…** keeps its word) and **⋯** (**On a failure**: **Pause** | **Go on**, a dot on the chosen one; **Sweep…**, **Save as…**, **Load**, **Clear finished**, and under **Stops and failures** **Kelvin replies at once**, a checkbox item, ticked or empty); the blocks in order, each two lines in its kind's colour (a scan, a step or wait, a script, a lab experiment): its number, name and state, then what it runs (a failed block: why it failed) and its tools, always faintly shown; a running block fills with its colour from the left as it goes, with "12/40 · 4:58 left" on its second line; a done block's name is dimmed, a skipped block faded; on a block: drag to reorder, duplicate, **again** (a finished one back in line), skip, remove; a click opens its settings in place (a script's values, a step's wait and set values, a scan's **Edit scan**) and the switch **Pause after** (at once; refused, it turns back and says why under itself). Under the blocks: **＋ Add** (it goes to the **Scan**, where the scans, scripts and experiments are); the **Step**, one line until it is used (a click on a kind opens its fields): **Set** a parameter to a value, **Wait** a time, or **Settle** (wait until a reading settles: within, for, a timeout), and **Add** puts it at the end; and **Paste a plan** (any item as written). **Scan** | **Sequence** is remembered by the browser: the pane opens on the one last shown. What the person does there (start, pause, stop, skip, remove, move or change a block, load, clear) is told to the chat it concerns, as a line in that chat and in a <lab_events> block before its next message: the chat that queued the block, or started the sequence; a block a person added concerns no chat, and every chat reads such changes with its next message. A block that fails is told the same way. With **Kelvin replies at once** on, a stop or a failure continues the chat it concerns at once.
- The **Runs** pane: **Scan** | **Sequence** at its top. **Scan**: the measurement now, the run in progress (a scan's, or a lab script's Kelvin runs), or the last one as it ended, until the next starts (its × puts it away); a run that starts opens the Scan, unless the pane shows the Sequence (then a dot on **Scan** says a run is on) or Runs would not fit beside the panels open (then the dot on **Runs**, or its Sequence count in the accent colour, says so, and nothing else on the screen moves). Under it, the functions: **New scan** (the scan editor), **Import script or VI…** (a script from the lab's scripts folders, each folder's under its name when there are several, or any file: its settings are found and shown, then a name and a colour; a LabVIEW VI chosen there, given a name, is translated into one of the lab's experiments at once, its review opening when it is done; a path Kelvin can't read is said in the dialog, the path kept; meanwhile it is listed among the experiments as translating, or with why it failed, then as a draft with **Review** until it is kept, and × to let it go after a yes, one that can't be kept too; **Cancel** or Esc right after **Add** lets the translation go on, with no dialog), a search, and the person's scans, scripts and the lab's experiments, one line each with ▶ (run it now: first in the sequence, its approval first, the Scan showing it as it runs), ＋ (last in the sequence) and its **⋯** (rename, colour, edit or versions, remove); a click on a scan opens it in the scan editor, on a script its settings (as its last run used them, else as in its file; typed, they go with its ▶ and ＋), on an experiment its action. Earlier runs are the **Analysis** pane's, whose head has **Report…** (also **For a lab notebook (ELN)**: a day's record). A run's view: its name and **Stop run**; how far it got, with the run's id at the end of that line, and plots as it measures; one row of buttons: **Report (PDF)**, **Analyze**, **Output**, **Script**, **CSV**, **Slides**, **Data (HDF5)**, **Commands**, **Excel**, **NumPy**, **Lab notebook** (the run's record as Markdown with its plots, for an ELN), **Open folder** (those the run has; what doesn't fit is under the row's **⋯**); the assessment in two lines (its chevron, or a click on it, shows the whole of it and the issues); a row that shows a part under it and hides it on a second press: **Key results**, **Plan**, **Run again**, **Table**. Each plot keeps a landscape shape (never taller than about 3:4 of its width, however many there are) and the plots fill the rest of the height. Key results and the whole assessment (Next, the issues) open by themselves where there is room, judged once when the run is first shown: when the view is 900 px or wider (the analysis then stands beside the plots, as on a 2560 × 1440 monitor, where Runs opens 960 px wide) and the window is not short, or when they fit above the plots and leave them at least 2/5 of their width in height. A finished run's **Run again** runs the same plan or script again, first setting back the settings it started with when ticked.
- The scan editor (**Edit scan** on a scan's block in the Sequence, **Edit** in a scan's ⋯ among the Scan's functions, a click on a scan there, or **New scan**), a panel of its own, as Special Measure shows a scan: the **Name**; **Loops** with how many (− the outermost goes, + one more outside the others) and a card for each, Loop 1 (the innermost, stepping fastest) first; a card: what it changes (an instrument, then its parameter, from two menus that tell two of one model apart by address; a **Variable…**; or **Time** for the innermost loop: every so many seconds, for so long), how it steps (**Range**: from, to, step, points; a **List** of values; a **Ramp**), **+ Change another** (a setting moving in step with it, from → to over its points), its **Wait** and **Start wait**, there and back, log spacing, and what it records (**+ Record**: the innermost at each point, an outer loop once per value of it); ↑ ↓ move a loop in or out, ✕ removes it. Then **Repeat**, **Setup** and **At the end** (an instrument and its setting from the same menus; a switch's value from its words; an empty one is only its button: **+ Computed**, **+ Setup**, **+ At the end**); what it can't show as fields stays **As written**. It says the points, the time and what is wrong as the person edits; **Save** keeps a new scan in the library, edited from the old one, which stays; **Run** and **Add to sequence** go through the queue and its approval; **Plan text** shows the plan. Closing it, or opening another scan, with changes asks: **Save**, **Discard** or **Keep editing**. Only people use it: you write plans with your own tools.
- The **Team chat** (the speech-bubbles icon): the lab's people writing to each other, kept on this computer for everyone who signs in here and their phones. Each browser sets the name it writes under (the pencil beside the name); a dot on the icon marks new messages. You read it with team_chat; you never write in it.
- The **Analysis** pane: a dataset (the list at the top) and what Kelvin's analysis subagent made of it. When a dataset is picked, the subagent (the chat's model, reading only that run, as the chat sees it) decides by itself how to process the data and how to show them: each figure it draws has its title, the plot, the processing steps it took (a few words each) and its fit's results, under one line on what the data show. A dataset is analyzed once; picked again, what was found shows, and **Analyze again** (at the top) asks anew. The pane has no fields: the person changes nothing there; to do an analysis another way, they ask you. A run's tab in the Runs pane has **Analyze**. Your analyze tool opens the pane with your work, shown the same way, its steps under it. Otherwise the pane shows the newest run, and moves to each new one as it ends (a run still measuring is analyzed once it ends), until the person picks a dataset. With no model to ask, it shows the dataset as measured.
- The **Terminal**: talk to Kelvin, or `/use <instrument>` (or `/<instrument>`) to send an instrument its own commands through the safety gate; `/kelvin` goes back.
- **Settings** (sidebar, at the bottom): **Account** (signed in with a Kelvin account: at the top, in a card of its own, the switch that lets the phone reach this Kelvin through the hub; it acts at once); **Models** (at the top, in a card of its own, the **Compare models** switch: on, **Compare** shows under the message box; off by default; it acts at once; then two columns in a wide window: the keys and plans, then the choices; API keys, the **ChatGPT plan**, a local server (**Local model**), **Other model services** (both fold under their titles; each service with **Test**: it asks the service for its models with its key, and says **Problem** when the service turns the key down or doesn't offer a model named for it, with the nearest it does; and its **Pictures**, **Auto**, **On** or **Off**, which acts at once), **Default model for new chats** (**Automatic** is Claude Opus), **Answer length**, **Second opinion on plans**); **Instruments** (two columns in a wide window: the instruments at the left, the **Lab owner's PIN**, **Lab roles** and **Safety changes go to the owner** at the right; setting up connected instruments when Kelvin starts, fetching manuals, the local network, writing cards; **Your lab's scripts**: the folders Kelvin runs scripts from, a row each with **Remove**, and **Add folder…** (**Lab** → **Set up…** → **Scripts…** and the first start add one too; the same folder twice is kept once, and one inside another listed is left out, as the outer one covers it), and the Python that runs them; what Kelvin installed for instruments, with **Remove**); **Data & reports** (the **Data folder**, with **Choose…**; **Chats' folders**; which reports to write); **Memory** (at the top, in a card of its own as it acts at once, the skills and recipes: a skill or recipe with **Show** (its steps) and its switch (Kelvin uses it; refused, it turns back and says why under the skill), a skill also with **Download**; **Add a skill…** takes one made for Claude; under it **Learn from conversations**, **Instructions for every conversation** and notes, which wait for **Save**); **Literature** (**Literature and web search**, a tick kept by **Save**); **Notifications** (**Webhook**, **Email** and **Heartbeat** each fold under its title; open or folded is kept in this browser); **Display** (**Page size**, at the top in a card of its own as it acts at once: the whole page smaller or bigger, 85% to 150% (+ greyed where the next size would leave the page under 560 px tall or under 360 px wide on this window, − and + at the ends, the tip and the line under the buttons saying why; a size too big for the window shows as the largest that fits, a line under the buttons saying which was chosen, and comes back on a bigger one; **Auto** with a mouse is held the same way), or **Auto**, which suits the window: 100% on a big or touch screen, 90% on a laptop, 85% on a short window; kept on that browser; in the Kelvin app also Ctrl + / Ctrl −, Ctrl 0 for Auto; **Approval cards in**: English or 简体中文, translated as shown, **English** on a card shows the original); **Phone** (**Phone access**, a switch in a card of its own at the top: it acts at once; under it the phones that reach Kelvin directly); **Updates**. In Settings a switch acts at once and a tick box waits for **Save**, as every other box and list does (a key's **Remove** too, greyed while nothing is set there: a key, the webhook's URL, the mail's password); in a row, a skill's switch and a service's **Pictures** act at once too; a button acts when pressed; the roles picked under **Lab roles** wait for **Save**, which sends them first (with the PIN when asked; refused, nothing else is saved and the reason shows under the roles; kept, they stay saved even if the rest is refused). While nothing waits, **Save** is greyed and the button beside it reads **Close**; with a change waiting it reads **Cancel** (which lets go of only what waits, never of what acted at once), and a dot after a section's name shows where the change is. × or Esc with a change waiting asks first whether to discard the changes on those sections (**Discard**, or **Keep editing**); while **Save** is on its way nothing closes Settings. Enter in a box presses the button on its own row (**Connect**, **Add**, **Change**, **Use this folder**) and never closes Settings; in **Current password** or a service's name or address it goes on to the next box, and in an empty **New password** it does nothing (on a local account, **Change** with no new password takes the password off, after asking **Remove your password?**). A refused **Save** keeps Settings open with the reason, and the changes. No dialog is taller than the window: its title and buttons stay, and what is between them scrolls.
- **Project settings**: **Instructions**, **Who may see this project** (**Confidential**: local models only, no literature or web search, never through the Kelvin hub; or which models, and literature and web search, one by one; a lock marks such a project and its chats in the sidebar), sharing on the Kelvin hub, **Notes**, **Skills and recipes**, **Devices**, **Master report**, **Reading list**.
